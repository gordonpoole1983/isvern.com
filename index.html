The person behind Isvern

Gordon Poole

Founder and Director

Gordon is a senior strategic and financial communications adviser with nearly two decades of experience guiding public and private companies through some of their most consequential moments. While he has a particular depth of expertise in the natural resources sector, his experience extends across a range of industries, advising businesses wherever complex narratives, demanding stakeholders, and high-stakes decisions intersect.

A qualified solicitor in the UK, Gordon brings a rare combination of legal rigour and commercial instinct to every mandate. He currently serves as a Special Adviser to the Paris Peace Forum's Global Council for Responsible Transition Minerals, reflecting a long-standing commitment to the responsible development of the sector.

Isvern was founded on a simple belief: that the best communications counsel is senior, experienced, and fully invested.
