Senior Leadership

Gordon Poole

Founder and Managing Director

Gordon is a senior strategic and financial communications adviser with two decades of experience guiding public and private companies as well as individuals through some of their most consequential moments. Gordon has spent his career working in London and Europe advising global businesses and has particular expertise in natural resources, latterly leading the Mining team for over 10 years at Camarco, part of APCO Worldwide, a global advisory, advocacy, and public relations firm. His network and influence extends to all the major capital markets, helping him advise international businesses wherever complex narratives, demanding stakeholders, and high-stakes decisions intersect.

A qualified solicitor in the UK, Gordon brings a rare combination of legal rigour and commercial instinct to every mandate. He currently serves as a Special Advisor to the Paris Peace Forum's Global Council for Responsible Transition Minerals, reflecting a long-standing commitment to the responsible development of the sector.
